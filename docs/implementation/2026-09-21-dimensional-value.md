# Dimensional QR artwork and campaign kits

The product direction is scannable QR artwork with a 3D appearance: sculpted materials, bevels, depth and lighting in an ordinary image. It does not require a 3D model, a special scanner or AR. Avoid claiming that competitors cannot do this or that appearance guarantees conversion or scan reliability.

## Delivered capability

- Four dimensional AI material directions: sculpted steel, optical glass, glazed ceramic and embossed paper. Existing botanical and illustrated modes remain selectable. Web input validation and the artwork worker share the same material catalogue. Generated artwork still goes through deterministic QR integration and scan checks.
- The landing exhibition keeps X first, followed by cobalt glass, iridescent folds and ceramic studies. These are unofficial brand examples, not partnerships.
- Generator → Campaign kit takes the current verified artwork into editable business, creator or agency message presets. The kit contains the original PNG, social square, vertical story, A6 card PNG and RGB PDF, a client review PDF, a scan report and a plain-text guide.
- The kit is prepared locally and is included with the studio. No upload, auto-publication, new payment or account is involved. Each PNG is decoded after encoding at full size and 50% size. Any mismatch blocks the entire kit. Cancellation and navigation abandon the pending download. Filenames and SVG text are bounded and escaped; image input is restricted to PNG data URLs.
- The report fingerprints each PNG and hashes the QR payload rather than repeating it in plaintext. QR images still expose their encoded content. The A6 PDF embeds the checked PNG; PDFs are not separately decoded. Print dimensions, compression risks, same-device social scanning, fixed QR content and hosting dependencies are explained in the guide.

| Agency | Consistent campaign outputs, client proof sheet and export scan record | Shareable client approval and batch personalisation |

Message presets are not a team workspace or an approval service. Do not describe those future outcomes as shipped. This increment is web only; existing mobile studio exports are unchanged.

## Offer direction

Keep useful free creation and permanent downloaded files. Test one-time artwork/credit purchases for occasional customers and subscriptions for recurring campaign operations. Existing Pro/Brand storage capacity and Stripe pricing are not changed by this increment. Unit costs, generation yield and willingness to pay must be measured before promising unlimited AI or choosing a final price. No legal claim to ownership of third-party marks or exclusive copyright in AI output is implied.

The competitor's own FAQ describes a seven-day connected-destination trial and separate API credits: https://qrcode-ai.com/en-US/custom-qr-code . This is evidence about its published offer, not its revenue or profitability.
