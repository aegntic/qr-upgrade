## Description

Make a QR code that feels like yours. QR Upgrade lets you create QR images for a website, Wi-Fi network or contact details, personalise the design, and check the result before sharing.

Create without an account. Your local draft stays on your device. Add your own artwork or choose a starting design, adjust the appearance, and export your finished QR image.

Use Scan Lab to explore how your design performs. Always test the final QR with your phone and at its intended print size before distributing it.

Optional links open the QR Upgrade website in your browser for account and online workspace features.
