# QR Upgrade
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product purpose
Create custom QR code images where artwork encodes a real destination. The user has explicitly rejected a product that only puts standard QR codes on coloured backgrounds. Preserve the generator features and page sections established from the supplied PDF.

## Users and workflow
People sharing business, social, product, Wi-Fi and contact destinations; brands and agencies preparing physical materials. Choose content, select Custom QR / Image QR / QR Art, personalise, validate and download. A profile photo can be included in the centre.

## Capabilities and constraints
The working web baseline includes local uploads, artwork templates, exact-content decoding and PNG/SVG/PDF exports. Live text-to-image generation, accounts, hosted dynamic links and analytics remain planned. Existing native source is a separate adaptation; do not claim a signed native release. No company-wide certification is established.

## Brand commitments
QR Upgrade / qrupgrade.com. On 18 September 2026 the user rejected the cream/green appearance and explicitly chose obsidian, glass and steel, with backlighting, shaders and excellent animation. Controls should feel like quality physical audio equipment. Existing generated QR artwork is approved visual material. The functional reference's styling is not binding.

## Accessibility
Keyboard-accessible editing, readable contrast, visible focus and reduced-motion alternatives. Never animate or distort the actual exported QR to achieve decorative effects.
