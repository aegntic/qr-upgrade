## Product overview

QR Upgrade turns destinations into artwork that scans. Its browser studio combines Custom QR, Image QR and QR Art with actual image decoding, editable composition and exports. It is a web application with optional connected account features. The studio can be used without signing in. Check `/billing` for current paid-plan availability; never infer a live offer from old planning prices.

## Audience and jobs

Initial audiences to validate: independent artists and musicians, cafés and venues, event teams, packaging designers and small creative agencies. They need a recognizable QR design, confidence before printing, and a consistent set of social and print files. The designer values creative control; the business owner values a correct destination and dependable handoff. Do not claim verified time or revenue savings without a measured case study.

## Positioning and differences

Category: artistic QR creator and campaign artwork studio. The demonstrated proposition is image-based design plus checks on the actual rendered output, followed by a coordinated campaign kit. Competitor comparisons require fresh, like-for-like testing. Conventional QR tools, design software with a QR pasted in and ordinary clickable links are alternative approaches, not proven inferior products.

## Current capabilities

- Custom patterns, uploaded images, prepared artwork and optional prompt-generated imagery.
- QR image validation before export, including reduced and simulated web views; this is not a guarantee for all phones or materials.
- PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG and RGB PDF exports; composed/artwork SVGs contain raster imagery.
- Explicit browser-local saves, history and library management. Clearing site data removes those copies.
- Account-dependent cloud saves, published content and dynamic redirects. Confirm live account availability before demonstration.
- Checked campaign-kit ZIP: original artwork, square social image, vertical story, A6 PNG/PDF, proof sheet and print guide.
- New source feature: an optional sharing card after export, created and checked locally. Its fixed creation link contains no user's destination; the QR image still carries its encoded payload.

## Limits and roadmap

No ISO verification, universal scan guarantee, CMYK proofing, fake scan percentages or invented performance numbers. Team workspaces, reusable brand presets and geographic routing remain planned. Native release artifacts and store listing availability require separate verification. Do not announce a native store launch or an Editors' Choice award without evidence.

## Objections and switching

“Will it scan?” Show the exact exported image, its decoder checks and a real phone/print test. “Will my work disappear?” Explain explicit local saves and optional cloud storage. “Can I change the destination?” Distinguish static encoded content from a published dynamic link. The likely switching tension is design freedom versus reliable scanning; validate that with actual users.

## Voice and identity

Use plain, confident language. Let the artwork and a working demonstration carry the claim. Visual identity: obsidian, glass, steel, cool backlighting, clear typography and restrained motion. Working campaign: **Scan the art.** Useful phrases: “Your destination. Your artwork.” and “Create it. Check it. Share it.” Avoid unsupported superlatives, fabricated reviews and promises of virality.

## Proof and customer language
