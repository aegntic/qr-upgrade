# Privacy, retention and suppliers — DRAFT FOR APPROVAL

Privacy owner, legal entity, applicable markets and approver: unassigned. The public privacy notice is a release deliverable requiring this review, not an automatically valid template.

Use the data inventory to document what is processed, purpose, location, recipients, controller/processor roles and retention. Collect only what a feature needs. QR contents can contain credentials/personal data even though encoding happens locally. Explain that exported QRs and shared files remain under the user's control and may expose their contents to anyone who scans them.

Confirm actual hosting access logs and subprocessors. Approve retention/deletion for those logs, security evidence and any future campaigns. Establish a verified contact and process for data access/correction/deletion requests, identity verification, tracking deadlines and explaining data the company does not hold. Do not collect extra credentials to respond to a privacy request.

Review suppliers before production use: service role, data access, region, security assurance, contract/DPA, transfer safeguards, breach notification, subprocessors, retention, deletion and exit/recovery. Review material changes and renewals. Where a supplier is only proposed, record it as proposed rather than publishing it as an active processor.

Before AI inference, analytics, precise/geographic routing, teams or persistent storage are added, complete a new data-flow review and impact-assessment screening, obtain required agreements, implement retention/access controls and approve updated notices/consent where applicable. A future payment integration should minimize payment-data scope using hosted checkout; HIPAA/PCI compliance must be scoped separately if those requirements become real.
