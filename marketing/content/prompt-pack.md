# QRUpgrade: seven production briefs

Prepared 21 September 2026. These are original, product-specific adaptations of the researched workflows. They are reusable task prompts, not instructions that have already been executed. Attach verified product facts, relevant source files and approved assets when starting a production task. Claims and service dependencies must be checked against the then-current product and tools.

**1. Search maintenance and opportunity report**

Use with the selected SEO operator. Reference: [SEO-GOD](https://github.com/AKCodez/seo-god).

> Investigate qrupgrade.com and produce an evidence-backed search improvement brief. First verify the current public site and the available product documentation. Record each observation with URL, date, method and confidence. Recheck `/docs`, sitemap destinations, canonical/indexability signals and the consistency of dynamic-link and saving claims; these were concerns on 21 September 2026, not assumptions about today.
>
> If authorized Search Console data is available, compare complete periods and segment material changes by query, page, country and device. Distinguish observed changes from possible causes. If access or history is missing, state the gap and continue with public evidence. Never substitute invented traffic or zero values for missing data.
>
> Select at most three improvements. For each, explain the user problem, evidence, proposed edit, affected URL, verification method and measurement date. Suggest a new page only if it serves a distinct useful intent with product proof. If evaluating AI visibility, label search-result presence separately from actual model responses and cited URLs.
>
> Deliver the ranked brief and proposed changes. Do not publish pages or enable recurring edits as part of this diagnostic task.

**2. “Scan the art” launch film**

Use with [promo-video-skill](https://github.com/AKCodez/promo-video-skill), then selected [Higgsfield skills](https://github.com/AKCodez/higgsfield-claude-skills) for surrounding creative.

> Create a reviewable production package for a 30-second QRUpgrade launch film. The core proof is a real QR artwork scanned by a real phone, opening the correct destination. Preserve the brand’s obsidian, glass, steel and cool lighting. Use genuine editor captures and working exports; identify any missing source footage before final rendering.
>
> Proposed structure: 0–3 seconds, reveal intriguing QR artwork; 3–10, show a concise real creation sequence; 10–20, demonstrate the successful scan and destination; 20–26, show two additional approved use cases; 26–30, show qrupgrade.com and one clear creation invitation. Adjust timings to match available proof.
>
> Deliver a script, shot list, asset manifest, captions and both 16:9 and 9:16 compositions. Keep essential action legible without sound. Use generated imagery only where it represents produced creative, and composite the validated QR without changing its geometry or quiet zone. Test the final demonstrated QR at the displayed size; print claims require a physical print test.
>
> Use only verified current features. Do not introduce unsupported awards, customer numbers, performance claims or store availability. Render when assets, approved service access and usage budget are available; otherwise return the exact missing inputs with the completed production package.

**3. Optional artwork-sharing feature**

Inspired by [App that markets itself](https://ariacodez.ai/resources/app-that-markets-itself). This adapts its sharing idea to the existing product rather than rebuilding an app.

> Design an optional sharing experience immediately after a successful, scan-checked QR export. The user should preview a separate showcase card containing their artwork, tasteful QRUpgrade attribution and a useful return link. Keep the ordinary functional export available without adding unwanted branding.
>
> Inspect the existing export flow first. Propose the smallest change that fits the current visual system. Define the mobile share-sheet behavior, download fallback, cancellation, unsupported browser, invalid scan, loading and failure states. Sharing must remain optional, with no forced login solely to dismiss it.
>
> Public remix links may contain only visual settings or assets the user deliberately chooses to publish. Do not expose original private destinations, Wi-Fi credentials, contact payloads or private artwork. If safe remix cannot be achieved simply, link to a generic creation page instead.
>
> Deliver a concise specification, preview concepts and an event plan. Distinguish export completed, share clicked, share sheet opened, measurable referral and referred creation completed. Do not treat share-sheet opening as a confirmed published post. State a testable adoption hypothesis without inventing expected conversion rates.

**4. One proof recording into a content week**

Inspired by [Content repurpose stack](https://ariacodez.ai/resources/content-repurpose-stack).

> Use the supplied real QRUpgrade demonstration as the factual source for a one-week content package. Extract the most useful moments with timecodes and a short claim ledger. Produce three short-video outlines, one six-panel carousel, one practical tutorial and five caption options. Each item should teach or demonstrate a distinct point.
>
> Lead with the viewer’s use case: an artist’s print, musician’s merchandise, event poster or café packaging, depending on what the footage actually supports. Avoid invented customer results. Keep the scan and correct destination visible where the post claims functionality. Identify the source footage behind every factual claim.
>
> Prepare English copy and localized drafts only for explicitly selected markets. Mark native-language review needs. Use actual channel analytics to recommend timing if available; otherwise label timing as an experiment. Deliver drafts and an asset checklist, with one useful CTA per item. Do not publish or send automatic messages as part of content preparation.

**5. International creator and business case-study research**

Adapted from the discovery structure in [openclaw-agent](https://github.com/AKCodez/openclaw-agent), with a different goal and factual constraints.

> Research up to ten potential QRUpgrade collaborators across the supplied cities or countries. Prioritize independent artists, musicians, cafés, events and packaging designers with a plausible public use for artistic QR codes. If no markets are supplied, propose three candidate markets with reasons and disclose the limits of the available evidence.
>
> For each prospect, provide the official website or public business profile, evidence of fit, the proposed QR use case, one tailored story angle and an appropriate public partnership channel if listed. Separate verified facts from creative suggestions. Do not invent names, review quotes, customer outcomes or audience statistics.
>
> Rank three opportunities and prepare private concept briefs. Explain what collaboration, assets and permissions would be needed before producing or publishing a real case study. Include a suggested way to measure the resulting product use. This task produces research and drafts; it does not contact people, deploy sites under their identities or imply an existing partnership.

**6. Creative experiments using actual results**

Inspired by [Meta ads + Claude](https://ariacodez.ai/resources/unlimited-meta-ads-claude) and [Viral reel decoder](https://ariacodez.ai/resources/viral-reel-decoder).

> Review the supplied QRUpgrade creative and authorized campaign or organic-post data. For each asset, record the hook, audience, format, placement, date range, spend where applicable, and available outcomes. Explain missing attribution and differences that prevent direct comparison.
>
> Propose three creative hypotheses around a real product demonstration, changing one main factor per comparison where practical. Define the primary measure as a meaningful product action, such as a completed export from an attributable visit. Use watch time, clicks and impressions as supporting evidence.
>
> State how audience, placement, budget, tracking, time and sample size affect interpretation. Do not declare a statistically reliable winner without an appropriate analysis. Do not treat a proprietary creative score or an unsourced retention threshold as a platform rule.
>
> Deliver the experiment brief, asset variants and a results table template. Any account changes, campaign creation or spending belong in a separately scoped execution task using verified integrations and an explicit budget.

**7. Reviewer and platform evidence pack**

Combines the useful presentation idea in [media-kit](https://github.com/AKCodez/media-kit) with QRUpgrade’s editorial objective.

> Build a factual QRUpgrade reviewer pack from the live product, approved demo assets and verified release information. Lead with the actual user benefit, then show the complete create → scan-check → export → real scan experience. Include a concise product description, current capabilities and limits, original screenshots, a short demonstration, founder background where supplied, and a clear review path.
>
> Separate the web product from each native platform’s verified status. Check the current official featuring or nomination guidance for each proposed store. Map readiness gaps to real work: reliability, accessibility, localization, onboarding, screenshots and release timing. Do not claim an Editors’ Choice award, endorsement or ranking that has not been received.
>
> Draft three distinct editorial angles supported by evidence and a brief suited to each relevant outlet category. Prioritize original case studies or a measurable product achievement over generic AI-launch language. Deliver a reviewable pack and nomination drafts. Do not send pitches or submit nominations during preparation.

Recommended order: search brief → proof film → content package → sharing specification → case studies → reviewer pack. Start paid creative experiments only when attribution and an approved budget exist. Use one shared product-facts document across every task.
