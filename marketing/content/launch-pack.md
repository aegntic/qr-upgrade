## Practical tutorial draft

**Make an artistic QR for a print project**

Start with a public destination you control. Open QR Upgrade and choose a prepared artwork or upload your own image. Adjust the composition while keeping the quiet zone clear. Wait for the studio checks to finish, and increase contrast or simplify the artwork if a check fails.

Export the finished file. For a coordinated set of layouts, open Campaign kit and edit the message once for the post, story and counter card. Check the exported code on a phone. If it will be printed, make a physical proof at the final size on the intended material; a glossy or curved surface can change how a camera sees it. Test again after a social platform compresses an uploaded image.

The code carries its destination. A static QR cannot be changed after export; use an appropriate managed link if updating the target later is part of the campaign. Never publish a QR containing private Wi-Fi credentials or contact information you do not intend to share.
