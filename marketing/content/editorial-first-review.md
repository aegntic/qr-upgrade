## Product Hunt draft

**Name:** QR Upgrade

**Tagline:** Turn your links into artwork that scans

**Description:** Create QR artwork from custom patterns, your own images or prepared art directions. Check the finished image before exporting, then carry it into social and print layouts with a campaign kit. Start in your browser without an account.

**Maker introduction:** We built QR Upgrade around a simple question: can the QR belong to the design? The studio lets you shape an image around a destination and checks the finished result before export. We'd like your feedback on the creation flow, scan checks and campaign handoff. Try a destination you control, export it and test it on your phone. For print projects, test a physical proof at the final size too.

Do not post this under an invented founder identity. The actual maker should review and publish it after the relevant live features are verified.

## Creative-tool pitch draft

**Working angle:** Making the functional QR part of the artwork.

QR Upgrade is a browser studio for creating QR artwork and checking the rendered result before export. The visual story follows one destination through image selection, composition, scan checks and a campaign kit for social and print. The proposed review material includes actual exported codes and a transparent explanation of what the digital checks do and do not prove.
